# 📊 Unemployment Analysis in India (COVID-19 Impact)

An Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA) project in Python analyzing the impact of COVID-19 lockdowns on unemployment rates and labor participation across Indian states.

## 📌 Key Insights
* **Lockdown Spike:** April–May 2020 saw peak unemployment rates across almost all states.
* **Pre vs. Post COVID:** Average Labour Participation Rate dropped sharply following the March 23, 2020 national lockdown.
* **Regional Disparities:** States like Haryana and Tripura recorded the highest mean unemployment rates.

## 🛠️ Tech Stack
`Python` • `Pandas` • `NumPy` • `Matplotlib` • `Seaborn`

## 📊 Visualizations Included
1. **Top 10 States Bar Chart:** Highest average unemployment rates by region.
2. **Monthly Trends:** Unemployment distribution month-by-month through 2020.
3. **Time-Series Chart:** Tracking unemployment trajectory for major states over time.
4. **Correlation Heatmap:** Relationship between Unemployment, Employment, and Labour Participation.
5. **Pre vs Post COVID Bar Comparison:** Labour Participation Rate before vs after March 2020.
